# Code of conduct

Spooler follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and patient, assume good intent, keep criticism about the work, and don't harass
anyone. Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that don't follow it, and
may block people who keep breaking it.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately through the e-mail on their
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/bloodf). Reports are kept confidential.
